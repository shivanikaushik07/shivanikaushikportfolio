## Project overview

A guided project-intake flow on growthjockey.com that qualifies leads quietly and books the first call. Seven screens, three questions, one CTA sitewide, ending in a slot on the team calendar rather than a form submission.

## A funnel with no filter

The old contact form collected a name and a paragraph. Every enquiry — from an ₹8 L brand refresh to a ₹2 Cr venture build — landed in the same inbox and earned the same 45-minute discovery call.

```quote
We're not short on interest. We're short on the right interest, early enough to act on it.
```

## Four decisions

```rows
One question at a time. Never two. | Each screen asks exactly one thing. Progress is visible, momentum is cheap, and abandonment points become measurable.
Start with a promise, not a form. | The landing screen sells the outcome behind a single CTA. No fields above the fold.
Qualify quietly. | Service line, company shape and a banded budget slider do the screening without feeling like a screening test.
Book decisively. | The flow ends in a booked slot, not a "we'll get back to you". The calendar is the handoff to sales.
```

## Identity, then company

The flow opens with a promise, then asks for identity and company on separate screens. Pill fields with floating labels, one gradient CTA, a Back link — and the greeting turns personal the moment a name exists.

## Qualify quietly

A three-step dot rail carries the rest: checkbox chips grouped by service line with Select All per group, then a budget slider per selected service, banded in lacs — so the answer is a range, never an interrogation.

## Where the leads land

Every completed intake drops into a single internal board. Sales opens one tab, not an inbox — leads arrive already tagged by service line, budget band and preferred slot, so the first triage takes seconds.

## And where they get scheduled

Booked slots flow straight from the intake into the team calendar, colour-coded by service line and owner. The growth lead rebalances the week in one glance, and moving a booked slot re-notifies the lead automatically.

## Numbers after 90 days

Measured against the old contact-form funnel over the same period.

```metrics
cols: 4
4× | Qualified leads per week
−72% | Discovery calls that went nowhere
5:12 | Median time to complete intake
83% | Completion rate after step one
```

Also true: total enquiries went down. The flow filters low-fit interest out before it costs anyone a call, which was the point. One question at a time is slower to fill and faster to finish — a promise converts better than a form, and the calendar, not the inbox, is where a lead becomes a client.
