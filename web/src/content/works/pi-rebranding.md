## Project overview

PI Industries — agriscience, crop protection, and life sciences. India-headquartered, operating across global markets through crop protection, custom synthesis (CSM), R&D, and distribution.

## Strategic challenge

The business had moved beyond agriscience. The brand hadn't. Deep legacy equity, outdated expression.

## Rebrand objectives

- Position PI as a science company, not just an agri company
- Build one coherent system across investor, partner, and regulator touchpoints
- Create room for expansion into health sciences and global R&D

## Target audience

- **Corporate** — investors, analysts, global CSM and R&D partners, regulators
- **Scientific** — researchers, technical experts, product development teams

## Audience insight

Farmers buy trust. Institutions buy governance. One brand had to carry both — so the system is layered, not flattened.

## Problem statement

A brand that read older than the company actually is, fragmenting across every new market it entered. Modernise without spending the institutional equity.

## Positioning

**A global science organisation advancing sustainable agriculture and health.**

Precise. Forward-looking. Calm authority, insight-led.

## Outcome

The identity shipped as a full system — masterbrand, three endorsed verticals, a sixteen-tone accent palette and a rollout kit spanning print, digital and environmental.

It went to work immediately on the largest stage the company had: PI presented under the new identity at **global trade events and exhibitions in Italy and Germany**, where the brand had to hold its own beside long-established European science companies. The architecture did what it was built for — one mark reading as a single global science organisation, with each vertical legible to the audience that needed it.
