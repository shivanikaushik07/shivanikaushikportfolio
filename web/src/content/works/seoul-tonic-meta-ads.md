## Project overview

A structured creative experiment for Seoul Tonic, an award-winning Australian recovery tonic built on Korean pear and hovenia dulcis, in partnership with a third-generation Korean pear farmer. Ten concepts across two markets, designed to find out which story the product should be told with before scaling spend behind any of them.

## The challenge

Most functional-beverage ads sell a benefit the buyer already wants. This one had to teach a behaviour. In Korea roughly two-thirds of people drink something before they drink alcohol; in the US and UK almost nobody does. The ad wasn't competing with other recovery products — it was competing with the idea that you just deal with the morning.

## Who it's for

Women 25 to 38 in major US and UK cities. Group fitness four or five times a week. Goes out regularly, weekdays and weekends. Has cancelled a morning session because of the night before and knows exactly how that feels.

```quote
She isn't choosing between the night out and the morning. She wants both, and she's already annoyed that she can't have them.
— The insight the whole set is built on
```

## One axis, two sides

Every concept sits on one side of a single question: is this product bought *before* the night or *after* it? That framing decides the audience, the moment and the shelf it competes on, so the set was built to answer it rather than to hedge.

```rows
Pre / Prevention | A lifestyle enhancer taken ahead of time. Seven of the ten concepts.
Post / Restoration | A recovery and digestion product taken after. Three concepts.
```

## Why Pre carries the weight

Reactive recovery is a crowded, low-loyalty category — it gets bought once, in a bad mood, and forgotten. Prevention is a routine, and routines repeat. Positioning the tonic as pre-emptive rather than reactive was the bet the majority of the budget went behind.

## Pre / Prevention

- **Show Up Everyday** · US · Static — split frame, last night's bar above, this morning's gym below, pouch as the connector. *"Big night. Big day. Nothing in between can stop you. You don't have to choose."*
- **Beyond-the-Moment Control** · AU · Static, two image variants — the tonic as a lifestyle enhancer, not a fix. *"Live beyond the moment."*
- **No Compromise Lifestyle** · AU · Static — leads with the Gordon Ramsay Food Stars win, because in-market credibility travels further than ingredient copy.
- **In-Flight Protection** · US · Static — pouch in the seat pocket, window glowing with first light. Depletion implied, never shown. *"Land fresh. Not recovering."*
- **Travel Energy Preservation** · US · Static — pouch placed among folded essentials like a curated carry-on. Sells the arrival, not the flight.
- **Synthetic-to-Natural Shift** · US · Carousel — capsules on one side, whole pears on the other, pouch as the outcome. *"No pills. Just pears."*
- **Active Digestion Support** · US · Static — the farmer and the brand together, in the orchard. *"Korea's recovery secret for generations. Now in America."*

## Post / Restoration

- **Natural Recovery** · AU · Static — a calm outdoor scene, pouch laid down rather than presented. *"Recovery, as nature intended it."*
- **Drinkability Drives Consistency** · US · Static — a traditional ACV bottle at 12% consumed beside the pouch at 100%. The argument isn't that the ACV is better, it's that this one actually gets finished. *"ACV made drinkable."*
- **Dining Experience Balance** · US · Static, two variants — hands reaching across a shared table, pouch as the quiet centre. Moves the product out of the hangover category entirely and into everyday digestion.

## Craft notes

No depletion is ever shown. No tired faces, no morning-after cliché, no before-and-after. The product sits in competent, well-lit lives — a gym bench, a seat pocket, a dinner table — because the audience doesn't see herself as someone who needs rescuing, and an ad that says otherwise loses her in the first second.

## What it moved

```metrics
cols: 3
+15% | Sales
+32% | Profile visits
1 | New product launched off the back of it
```

The profile-visit number is the one that matters most. At more than double the sales lift, it says the creative was doing category education, not just conversion — people were going to find out what this thing actually is before buying. That is exactly the behaviour a brand wants when it is teaching a market a new habit, and it is the reason a new product launch followed rather than preceded this work.
