## Project overview

Paid social for Bespoke Skin Technology, a dermatologist-founded skincare brand for women whose skin is changing. A dormant ad account, a large existing asset library from the founder, and no read on what actually converts. The job was to get spend moving again without shooting anything new, then turn the first weeks into a structured test.

## Two phases

- **Phase 1 — reactivate.** Build traction off the current asset library and get a directional read on which messaging resonates. Fifteen assets: eight videos, six statics, one carousel.
- **Phase 2 — test.** A/B across the variables that actually move cost per result: messaging, the three-second hook in both visual and verbal form, visual format, founder versus UGC, and CTA.

## Why founder-led first

Dr Katherine Armour has twenty years of dermatology behind her and the brand's whole voice is built on her being in conversation rather than making content. That's an unfair advantage on a feed full of before-and-afters, so Phase 1 leans entirely on her — no UGC until there's a founder baseline to test against.

```quote
She sounds like a dermatologist in conversation, not a dermatologist making content.
— Bespoke brand guidelines, the test every cut had to pass
```

## Naming convention

Every asset carries its own identification, so reporting can be sliced without opening a single file.

```note
BST_TRUSTB_ME_FLE_dreamcream_Edu01
brand · angle · platform · format · product · content type and sequence
```

Edu for educational, PS for the static and carousel set. Product sits in the name because Dream Cream and the Full System are different funnels with different buyers, and the first thing anyone will want from the data is which of the two is carrying the account.

## Content pillars

```rows
Founder-led educational | Katherine explaining one thing properly. The bulk of Phase 1.
Personal story | Why she built the brand. Trust before product.
Skin stages | Perimenopause, menopause, postmenopause, pregnancy. The brand's clearest territory.
Skincare simplified | The anti-multi-step argument. Nine steps become one.
Dermatologist-made | Formulation credibility, multiple concerns, one product.
Ingredient-led | Niacinamide, bakuchiol, DNA repair enzymes, liquorice root.
```

## Hooks in test

The first three seconds are the only variable that matters until they stop being the bottleneck. Phase 1 runs these as-is; Phase 2 splits each into a visual-first and a verbal-first cut.

- Menopausal skin: what I recommend
- What niacinamide actually does
- Unpopular dermatologist opinion
- I built a skincare brand for women in their 40s and beyond
- Your 9-step routine just became 1 step
- Every stage of you deserves skincare that keeps up

## Editing discipline

These are long-form founder clips, not ads. Each one carries an in and an out point to the second, a title card where the hook has to be carried verbally, and an end frame that names the product and the action. Nothing ships as a raw export.

## Three things the plan needed fixing

- **Duplicate identifier.** *fullsystem_Edu03* is assigned to two rows — the 40s video and the personal-story cut — while the second one's export is named Edu05. Reporting would have merged two different creatives.
- **Wrong source link.** Both skin-stages statics point at the "too many products" video file rather than static source.
- **Typo in an export name.** *dreancream_Edu04*. Small, but it breaks any naming-based filter in Ads Manager.

## Results

Phase 1 ran off the existing asset library with no new production. Every number below came from recutting footage the brand already owned.

```metrics
cols: 3
50 | Units of Dream Cream sold
+40% | Website visits
+15% | Instagram profile views
```

The profile-view lift matters more than it looks. Founder-led creative sends people to check whether the person in the video is real before they buy — so a rise in profile views alongside site traffic says the trust pillar is doing its job, not just the product pillar.

Phase 2 now has a baseline to test against: hook rate by pillar, founder versus UGC, and whether Dream Cream or the Full System carries the account.
